Endfield Design System

The look for everything clockmost builds next — the dashboards, the phone launcher, the reports. Not a copy of the promo site; a study of the game's interface: a near-black ground, hairline outlines with two corners cut away, the diamond as the marker, one industrial yellow doing the structural work, and the magenta / cyan / yellow triband reserved for one signature moment. Restraint is the point. This page is the reference and endfield.css in one file.

SEC.00

Overview

Endfield's HUD reads like field equipment — a survey rig, a factory console. Thin lines, lots of black, and every mark looks like it reports a value. Four rules carry it.

Restraint first

Hairline outlines, not filled boxes. Negative space over decoration. One filled element per group — the primary action. The promo website is louder because it's marketing; the game itself is quiet.

Yellow is structure

The one accent — #FFFA00 — behaves like safety-yellow on a machine: a frame, a rail, a platform edge, the active state. It is rarely the fill of a button and never body text.

Cut, don't round

Radius 0. The character comes from a chamfer that removes the top-right and bottom-left corners of panels, cards and buttons. Corner brackets frame anything in focus. The diamond ◇ is the marker.

Two surfaces

HUD — the dark operating plane for tools and dashboards. DOC — the light paper plane for reports (used on official operator art). Same tokens, swapped values. Toggle in the header.

The 3 : 1 : 1 ratio

YELLOW · 3CYAN · 1MAGENTA · 1

Yellow does the structural work. Cyan means a healthy reading. Magenta means look here. If a screen looks evenly tri-coloured it is wrong — that mix is the triband, a brand mark used once per view.

Do

Let yellow carry the structure and the focus state. Reach for cyan only to say "this reading is fine", magenta only to say "attention". Keep the ground genuinely dark.

Don't

Balance the three colours. Fill panels with accent. Set body text in yellow. Put the triband on more than one element in a view. Use yellow as text on the DOC ground — it fails contrast there; it becomes fill-only.

Restraint

One orchestrated moment — the boot — plus quiet scroll reveals. Everything else is instant state. Every animation must no-op under prefers-reduced-motion.

SEC.11

Voice

Words are design material. The system talks like field telemetry: terse, present-tense, specific.

Sounds right

SYSTEM NOMINAL · 31 CONTAINERS
OP_MODE: ACTIVE · SECTOR CLEAR
backup full — done (rc=0)
Deploy now → "Deployed"

Doesn't

"Everything looks good! 🎉"
"Oops — something went wrong."
"Please wait while we process…"
"Are you sure you want to…"

Vocabulary

  • [SEC.NN] · [SYS] · [ OK ] — bracketed tags in mono
  • // — separator and comment prefix
  • OP_MODE · PWR · NODE · SECTOR — screaming-snake field keys
  • dates as YYYY.MM.DD, times with a trailing Z
  • buttons state the outcome; toasts confirm it. No apologies, no vagueness.
